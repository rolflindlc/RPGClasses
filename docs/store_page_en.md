# Rolflin's Rites & Paths

**Choose your race. Walk your path. Complete the rites.**

Rolflin's Rites & Paths adds a balanced class and race system to Minecraft Forge 1.20.1, built so that anyone can
change it with datapacks.

> **Beta:** balance is still being tuned. Back up your world and report anything odd.

## Features

- **8 classes:** Barbarian, Bard, Cleric, Mage, Monk, Paladin, Ranger and Rogue. Each has branches, perks and
  subclasses (24 in total), and each fights in its own way.
- **12 races** with **39 subraces:** Centaur, Colossus, Draconid, Dwarf, Elf, Goblin, Human, Infernal, Orc, Slime,
  Triton and Undead. Climb your race's ranks by completing **rites** at the Race Altar, including rite bosses.
- **Bard:** a rhythm minigame with four lanes. Land notes to build a combo and power a music school with its own
  instruments, sheet music and auras for your allies.
- **Cleric:** a holy healer who blesses, purifies and shields allies, holds up a Sanctuary and saves them from the
  final blow with Miracle. Fly as an Angel, heal with your own blood as a Martyr or pray through a guardian angel as
  an Intercessor.
- **A hidden experimental class:** find the secret on the class screen to unlock the Spin Master, who throws
  spinning balls of ten materials that bounce, drill into enemies and come back to the hand, building up Spin until
  the Golden Rectangle, a cinematic ultimate. Its balance is not guaranteed.
- **Allies:** mark your allies (key J) and share auras and effects with them.
- **Beast Master bonds**, a Mage with free-cast slots, a Chronomancer who rewinds and stops time, and much more.
- **Class and race HUD** with cooldowns and key bindings.
- **Fully data-driven:** classes, branches, races, subraces and rites are defined in datapacks. Edit, remove or add
  your own.
- **Translated** into English, Spanish, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Russian, Brazilian Portuguese, German,
  French, Japanese and Italian.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft **1.20.1**, Forge **47.2** or newer.
- Optional: **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks**, **Epic Fight** and **Curios**. The mod runs without them, and unlocks
  extra content when they are present.

## For modpack makers

You may include the mod in your modpack as long as you credit the author (Rolflin) and link to the official page.
The mod must be unmodified. It must not be used on pay-to-win or pay-to-fast servers, or on servers or distributions
that discriminate. See the license for the full terms.

## For addon makers

Add new classes, subclasses, races, abilities and rites with datapacks, or build addons that depend on the mod.
Addons may be monetized, as long as they do not give pay-to-win or pay-to-fast advantages and do not copy the mod's
code or assets.
